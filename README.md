# talks
Talks and lectures I've delivered .

### Contents

_Most Recent First_

1. [Endpoint Security - Engineering Cybersecurity Solutions](pdf/Engineering%20Endpoint%20Security%20Solutions%20%28Oct%2C%202025%29.pdf)

	_October, 2025_ 
	
	This is a revised version of the lecture given in 2024 for the MTU Cyber Innovate class of 2025-26. The 2025 update expands coverage of endpoint security within a growing threat landscape, adds discussion of cybercrime costs and attack trends, and refreshes the AV/EDR/ransomware engineering sections while keeping the deeper endpoint engineering focus.

2. [A Tour of Cybersecurity - AI Native Security](pdf/A%20Tour%20of%20Cybersecurity%20-%20AI%20Native%20Security.pdf)

	_April, 2025_ 
	
	I delivered this talk at the [Lero Research Centre](https://lero.ie/) 'Industry Day 2025' conference held at the [University of Limerick](https://www.ul.ie/), which was attended by over 200 delegates. The theme of the conference was _'trustworthy AI-enable systems'_ so after a general overview, the presentation emphasizes the growing impact and costs of cybercrime, the need for effective detection, and the emerging importance of AI in defending against threats.



3. [Endpoint Security - Engineering Cybersecurity Solutions](pdf/Engineering%20Endpoint%20Security%20Solutions.pdf)

	_September, 2024_ 
	
	In this lecture, which I delivered to a class of Cybersecurity PostGraduate students at [MTU](https://www.mtu.ie), I talk about the endpoint security space before delving deeper into the _ins_ and _outs_ of engineering solutions for the endpoint. In particular, I use the development of a remediation-rollback solution as a case study. 



4. [Engineering Security Solutions - Rollback Remediation](pdf/Engineering%20Security%20Solutions%20-%20Rollback%20Remediation.pdf)

	_November, 2023_

	This is a talk I gave at the [Cork|Sec Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/corksec/) (a local industry event) where I discuss engineering in cybersecurity. After a general introduction, I describe how AV works before I delve into the details of engineering a ransomware rollback solution.
